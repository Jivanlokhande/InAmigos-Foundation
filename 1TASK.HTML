About Us

Building a kinder, more equal society

Founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organisation licensed by the Central Government, headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. What began as a local initiative has grown into a nationwide network of professionals, volunteers, and corporate partners.

We work across Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and beyond — spanning 28 states — through a programmatic, ground-level approach to social change. Our internship and volunteering programmes have also given thousands of young people hands-on experience in real-world social impact work.

Our Projects

Six causes, one ecosystem of care

Each project targets a distinct gap on the ground — together they form a complete approach to community development.

S

Project Seva

Distributing food and clothing to underprivileged communities, so no one sleeps hungry or unclothed.

B

Bachpanshala

Ensuring quality education for underprivileged children, nurturing young minds toward brighter futures.

J

Project Jeev

Animal welfare through rescue, protection, and feeding drives for the voiceless in our communities.

U

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development and pathways to financial independence.

P

Project Prakriti

Environmental conservation and sustainability efforts — planting the seeds of a better tomorrow.

V

Project Vikas

Building employability through skill-development and internship programmes for young professionals.

Social Impact

Change you can see, on the ground

From feeding drives to classrooms, every project translates into a tangible shift in someone's daily life — dignity restored, a child taught to read, an animal fed, a woman earning her own income.
